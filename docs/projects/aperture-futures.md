## Overview

Aperture Futures is an AI-powered advisory platform for UN Security Council intelligence. The product combines three experiences — BlueLines (resolution research and drafting), LiveLines (meeting analysis), and CrossLines (negotiation simulation) — on a Retrieval-Augmented Generation backbone so insights stay grounded in official UNSC documentation. On this portfolio, the highlighted contribution is frontend development: responsive interfaces, reusable components, modern layouts, and UX polish across product surfaces used by diplomatic and research audiences.

## Challenges and solutions

Contribution-scoped challenges for the frontend work, with product context from digowl.me:

1. **Dense intelligence UX** — Diplomatic users need fast access without clutter. Solution: clean professional layouts, strong hierarchy, and reusable components for search, document viewing, and product switching.
2. **Cross-device access** — Stakeholders work from offices, conferences, and field locations. Solution: responsive layouts and touch-friendly navigation across breakpoints.
3. **Trust in AI surfaces** — Users must see sources, not opaque answers. Solution: UI patterns for citations, document references, and verification-friendly reading (aligned with the product’s RAG attribution model).
4. **Three products, one system** — BlueLines, LiveLines, and CrossLines share brand and interaction language. Solution: shared component patterns and consistent navigation/layout systems.
